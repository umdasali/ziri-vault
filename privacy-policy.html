KryptSafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · App: KryptSafe (com.kryptsafe) · Publisher: Ziriverse

KryptSafe is a private, encrypted vault for photos, videos and documents. It is designed so that your data stays on your phone and is never available to us or anyone else.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.
  • KryptSafe has no internet permission and cannot connect to any network.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Information we collect

None. Ziriverse does not receive any information from the app. Nothing you add to KryptSafe, and nothing about how you use it, is sent to us.

Files you add

Files you import are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM and stored only in the app's private storage. They are decrypted in memory only while you view them. Encrypted files never leave your phone unless you use the Restore (export) feature to save a decrypted copy to a location you choose. Exported copies are not encrypted, and you are responsible for protecting them.

Your PIN, passphrase and fingerprint

Your PIN or passphrase is never stored. It is used to derive an encryption key (using Argon2id), which protects your vault key. That key is additionally protected by the Android Keystore on your device. If you enable fingerprint unlock, authentication is handled entirely by Android; KryptSafe never receives or stores your fingerprint data.

We cannot recover your PIN, passphrase or files. If you forget your credential, or if you enable "Erase after failed attempts" and the limit is reached, your vault is permanently unrecoverable.

Permissions

  • No internet permission. The app cannot send data off your device.
  • Photos, videos and files: accessed only through the Android picker for items you choose to import, and to remove the originals if you ask.
  • Biometric: used only to unlock the app, through Android's system prompt.

Third parties

KryptSafe contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we share no data with third parties. The app includes open-source components for video playback and encryption; these run entirely on your device.

Children

KryptSafe is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

All your data is stored on your device. Deleting items in the app removes them from the vault. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, permanently deletes everything. We hold no copy and have nothing to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
